FAQ

Which phones?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later.

Is it free?

Yes. Reffi is free on the App Store, and there is nothing to sign up for.

Do I need an account?

No. Reffi works without an account, and your fridge stays on your phone.

Where do the recipes come from?

A curated library of 250 recipes from 12 cuisines plus your own, ranked by what spoils first. Reffi does not generate recipes with AI.
